Questions

Is this legal advice?

No. Trademark Watch reports publication facts from USPTO's own data — a new filing exists that matches your saved criteria. It doesn't assess likelihood of confusion, validity, or infringement. For any of that, talk to a trademark attorney.

Where does the data come from?

Official USPTO systems — the Trademark Status & Document Retrieval (TSDR) API and USPTO's public bulk trademark data. Nothing scraped, nothing unofficial.

When does this launch?

We're building now and targeting a 2027 launch. Early-access signups get first access and a founding-member rate.
