Questions

What does "white-label" mean here?

Alerts, digests, and reports go out carrying your firm's name and logo — Trademark Watch isn't mentioned to your clients. You decide which clients and marks are watched; we handle the monitoring and delivery.

Can I use it for my own marks without a firm?

Yes. The Starter and Growth plans are for founders and small businesses watching their own marks directly. Same monitoring, just without the branding layer.

Is this legal advice?

No. Trademark Watch reports publication facts from USPTO's own data — a new filing exists that matches saved criteria. It doesn't assess likelihood of confusion, validity, or infringement. That judgment stays with you and your clients' counsel.

Where does the data come from?

Official USPTO systems — the Trademark Status & Document Retrieval (TSDR) API and USPTO's public bulk trademark data. Nothing scraped, nothing unofficial.

When does this launch?

We're building now and targeting a 2027 launch. Early-access firms get first access, a say in the client-facing reports, and a founding-partner rate.
